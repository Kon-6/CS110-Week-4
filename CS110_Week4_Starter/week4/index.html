Film Review

Form and Void Act 2: The Beholder

Form and Void Act 2: The Beholder was interesting because it had a mysterious feeling that made me want to keep watching. I liked how the visuals and music helped create the mood of the film. One specific detail that stood out to me was the large stone-style Form and Void title because it made the film feel dramatic and serious. Overall, I think the film did a good job using its visuals and sound to keep my attention. It caught my attention making me wonder what the plot was about but it has no clear plot, that's up to your imagination.
